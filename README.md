# 🚀 ASTRA-VITAL

### AI-Assisted Health Intelligence for Astronauts

ASTRA-VITAL is a software prototype designed to help astronauts monitor changes in their health during long-duration space missions.

The system learns an astronaut's personal health baseline, analyzes multiple health indicators, detects unusual changes, and provides explainable alerts and decision-support recommendations.

## 🎯 Problem

Long-duration space missions can expose astronauts to various physiological and behavioral challenges. Detecting meaningful changes in health early can be difficult when astronauts are far from Earth.

## 💡 Our Solution

ASTRA-VITAL combines:

- Personal Health Baseline
- Multi-indicator Health Monitoring
- Anomaly Detection
- Explainable Health Alerts
- Mission-aware Decision Support

## 🛰️ NASA Data

NASA open data and research resources will be investigated and integrated where scientifically relevant to support the system's spaceflight health context.
